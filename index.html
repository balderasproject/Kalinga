About Kalinga

Care, Protection, and Nurturing

Kalinga is a community-centered stray pet adoption initiative serving Mamburao and nearby towns in Occidental Mindoro. We give homeless, abandoned, or stray dogs and cats a safe temporary shelter and match them with loving forever families.

Why Kalinga Was Built

Previously, animal rescue efforts in Mamburao relied entirely on handwritten logbooks, scattered social media posts, and physical shelter visits. Interested adopters from distant barangays faced major travel hurdles, and staff struggled with duplicate records. Kalinga digitizes the entire workflow.

Our Core Objectives

  • Centralized digital database for rescued animal profiles & medical records.
  • User-friendly public interface to browse pets and submit applications online.
  • Streamlined administrative screening and post-adoption welfare tracking.

Serving Mamburao & Neighboring Barangays

From Brgy. Payompon and Poblacion to Balansay, Tayamaan, and Fatima — our volunteers and municipal partners work tirelessly to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome stray animals across Occidental Mindoro.
