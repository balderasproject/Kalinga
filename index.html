About Kalinga

Care, protection, and nurturing for stray and abandoned animals in Mamburao, Occidental Mindoro.

Our Core Purpose

Transforming Stray Animal Welfare in Occidental Mindoro

Kalinga was established to bridge the gap between rescued strays and compassionate families. Historically, local rescue efforts relied on manual paper logbooks and scattered social media posts. The Kalinga: Stray Pet Adoption System introduces a centralized digital platform to streamline rescue records, adoption applications, and shelter operations.

Protection & Care

Safe temporary shelter and veterinary treatment.

Transparent Process

Clear online application and visit scheduling.

Key Project Objectives

  • Centralized digital database for animal profiles & medical records.
  • Public web interface to browse pets and submit applications electronically.
  • Administrative module for shelter staff to screen and review requests.
  • Post-adoption tracking to ensure lifelong welfare and support.
